## What actually separates the methods

Best accuracy reached by each *family* at each GRIDE budget. The families are:

- **LS-corrected** — the consuming layer is rebuilt by the closed-form least-squares solve with an intercept, against the dense network's target (seed strategies 01/07/08/10 and every `N*`).
- **merging** — neurons are replaced by cluster barycentres (03/05/06/09).
- **prune / OT-transport** — magnitude pruning (02) and the Intra-Fusion OT baseline (04).
