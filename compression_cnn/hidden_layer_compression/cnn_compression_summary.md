## Does the intrinsic dimension predict compressibility?

The Zador argument says a layer's achievable width is set by its *intrinsic* dimension, so the compressible fraction of a network should track `N/d` — how many channels it carries per dimension it actually occupies — rather than the dataset's difficulty or the model's size. ## Where the savings are
